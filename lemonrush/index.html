LEMON RUSH

10 days, 30 seconds each. Highest total profit wins.

SCORE €0.00 DAY 1/10 CASH €50.00 CUPS LEFT 40 TIME 30 SUNNY
Passers-by walk along the back. Your line waits up front behind the red rope. Patience. When it runs out, they leave. yum!Happy. Jumps and tells friends tomorrow. too priceyLost customer, with the reason.

Tap the stand, this button or press Space to pour. You can pour ahead: up to 3 cups wait on the counter, and the first person in line takes one. Every cup you pour uses supplies, even if nobody buys it. The sun shows how much of the day is left.

After you play

  1. Which single change gave you the biggest jump in profit? How sure are you it was that change and not the weather?
  2. Did your biggest leak move after you fixed it? What does that tell you about where to spend money first?
  3. Which upgrade did you buy that did nothing? Why did it feel like a good idea at the time?
  4. Did upselling and cross-selling pay off, or did they slow your line down?
  5. Your own venture: where do customers drop out today? Notice, wait, price or supplies?
