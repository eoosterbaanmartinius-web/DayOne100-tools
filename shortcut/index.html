SHORTCUT

Run FitPal for 8 months. Grow fast, or build a company you would defend on the front page.

You just launched FitPal

A student fitness app. It uses your phone's location, a selfie to track progress, and AI to set daily goals. A free version with ads, and a premium plan for €9.99 a month.

Every month brings one decision. Some choices bring fast money. Some come back to bite you later. Nobody tells you which, until it happens.

WATCH FOUR METERS

Users and cash are your growth. Trust is what users think of you: low trust makes them leave. Risk is how interesting you are to regulators and journalists.

THE PRESSURE

You have 20 seconds per decision. Too slow, and your growth lead decides for you (always the shortcut). Your investor sets targets. Every shortcut is a secret. It stays buried for two months, then the press can find it: a shortcut in month 1 can blow up in month 3. The higher your risk, the bigger the chance. Even one secret is enough.

WATCH YOUR MONEY

Cash can go below zero: then you are in debt and the bank charges 5% interest a month. If your debt goes past €10,000, the bank pulls the plug and FitPal is bankrupt. Game over.

HOW YOU WIN

Highest company value after 8 months. Value = cash + users × trust. Earn up to five stars and unlock badges.

No login. Nothing is stored, except your best score in this browser. FitPal is a fictional company; the "In real life" cases are real.
